# SEO Setup Guide for OGO Technology - Manager Pro

This document outlines the SEO implementation for managerpro.ogotechnology.net to help it appear in Google search results when people search for "OGO Technology".

## ✅ What Has Been Implemented

### 1. **Meta Tags** (`index.html`)
- Primary SEO meta tags (title, description, keywords)
- Open Graph tags for social media sharing
- Twitter Card meta tags
- Canonical URL
- Language and region tags

### 2. **Structured Data (JSON-LD)**
- SoftwareApplication schema
- Organization schema
- WebApplication schema
- Helps Google understand your content better

### 3. **Sitemap** (`public/sitemap.xml`)
- Lists all main pages of the application
- Helps search engines discover and index your pages
- Accessible at: https://managerpro.ogotechnology.net/sitemap.xml

### 4. **Robots.txt** (`public/robots.txt`)
- Allows all search engines to crawl your site
- Points to the sitemap location
- Accessible at: https://managerpro.ogotechnology.net/robots.txt

## 🚀 Next Steps to Get Indexed by Google

### 1. **Submit to Google Search Console**
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Add your property: `https://managerpro.ogotechnology.net`
3. Verify ownership (DNS, HTML file, or meta tag)
4. Submit your sitemap: `https://managerpro.ogotechnology.net/sitemap.xml`

### 2. **Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools**
1. Go to [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters)
2. Add your site
3. Submit your sitemap

### 3. **Verify Files Are Accessible**
After deployment, verify these URLs are accessible:
- ✅ https://managerpro.ogotechnology.net/sitemap.xml
- ✅ https://managerpro.ogotechnology.net/robots.txt
- ✅ https://managerpro.ogotechnology.net/ (main page)

### 4. **Request Indexing**
After submitting to Google Search Console:
1. Use the "URL Inspection" tool
2. Enter your homepage URL
3. Click "Request Indexing"

### 5. **Create Backlinks**
- Link from your main website (ogotechnology.net) to managerpro.ogotechnology.net
- Share on social media
- Add to business directories
- Include in email signatures

### 6. **Content Optimization**
- Ensure your content includes relevant keywords naturally
- Use "OGO Technology" in page titles and descriptions
- Create quality content that provides value

4. **Page Speed**: Ensure fast loading times (already optimized with Vite)
5. **HTTPS**: Make sure your site uses HTTPS (required for good SEO)
